COMPUTER SCIENCE AND BUSINESS SYSTEMS DEPARTMENT

The Department of Computer Science and Business Systems was established in the academic year 2022-23 with intake of 60. The vision of the department is to build competent, industry-ready professionals to largely contribute the society, by imparting the knowledge in the domains of computer science and business systems. The department has a team of well-qualified, highly experienced faculty members and world class infrastructure sufficient to foster the development of the young mind for global fulfillment.

Curriculum & Industry Preparedness

This emerging course strives to impart knowledge of cutting-edge technologies and business skills through practical experience to make the students industry-ready. The fundamental objective of this course is to ensure that the students have a solid foundation in computer science as well as to develop an equal interest in and appreciation for humanity, value-added science, and other interrelated topics.

Moreover, the student will be gained experience for the workplace through the use of cutting-edge subjects like computational statistics, software development models, design thinking, cognitive science & analytics, machine learning, financial management, services science and service operational management, marketing research, and marketing management.
